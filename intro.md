# Vandor Labs

Build starships and sci-fi bases in **Minecraft Forge 1.12.2**. Vandor Labs adds
customisable building blocks, animated displays, working mechanisms and
redstone-controlled lighting and effects.

- **Build:** [hull panels, blocks, slabs, stairs, ramps, diagonal walls, pipes, vents, padding, glass and portholes](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/building.md).
- **Furnish:** [storage](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/programmable-storage.md), [chairs and connected seating](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/chairs.md), and [extendable landing gear](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/version-1.1.md#landing-gear).
- **Display:** [planet and ship viewscreens, animated bridge/engineering panels, consoles and interactive inputs](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/programmable.md).
- **Open:** [configurable doors, including large double doors](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/doors.md), [trapdoors](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/programmable-trapdoor.md) and [diagonal hatches](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/programmable-diagonal-trapdoor.md), with sliding/rotating motion and joined groups.
- **Drive:** [Pilot Seat and Taxi Mode](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/pilot-seat-taxi.md) turn a connected craft into a ground vehicle, preserving its blocks and settings when parking.
- **Enclose:** [Canopies and adjustable front windows](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/canopies.md) offer sliding/hinged motion, glass or hull materials and redstone control.
- **Move:** [deployable ramps, escalators, stairs, lifts and extending platforms](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/ramp-controller.md).
- **Illuminate:** [programmable lights, light slabs and frames](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/lights.md), plus [thrusters, ion drives, plasma vents, impulse engines and hover effects](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/propulsion.md).
- **Control:** [buttons, switches, levers, sliders, aircraft throttles, screen controls and triggers](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/controls.md), linked by redstone channels carrying levels 0â€“15.
- **Customise:** edit settings with the [Configurizer](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/CONFIGURIZER.md), copy them with the [Duplifier](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/DUPLIFIER.md), and choose [materials or custom artwork](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/unified-materials.md).
