# Vandor Labs

[![Watch the Vandor Labs demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rheide/vandorlabs/master/docs/images/demo-preview.jpg)](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs#readme)

**[Watch the one-minute demo](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs#readme).**

Build starships and sci-fi bases in **Minecraft Forge 1.12.2**. Vandor Labs adds
customisable building blocks, animated displays, working mechanisms and
redstone-controlled lighting and effects. **Version 2.2** expands your workshop
with modular landing gear, rotating platforms, industrial multiblocks and company designs.

- **Build:** [hull panels, blocks, slabs, stairs, ramps, diagonal walls, pipes, vents, padding, glass and portholes](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/building.md). Share artwork across matching faces with Tile, Stretch or aspect-preserving Fit layouts.
- **Furnish:** [configurable storage](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/programmable-storage.md), plus [chairs and connected seating](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/chairs.md).
- **Display:** [planet and ship viewscreens, animated bridge/engineering panels, consoles and interactive inputs](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/programmable.md).
- **Open:** [configurable doors, including large double doors](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/doors.md), [trapdoors](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/programmable-trapdoor.md) and [diagonal hatches](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/programmable-diagonal-trapdoor.md), with sliding/rotating motion and joined groups.
- **Drive and hover:** [Pilot Seat vehicle modes](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/pilot-seat-taxi.md) turn a connected craft into a Taxi or equipped Hover vehicle, preserving blocks and settings when parking. Add [Autopilot and Waypoints](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/vehicle-autopilot.md) for planned routes; sprint accelerates hover travel with engine-specific exhaust.
- **Deploy landing gear:** combine standard or Large [fixed Mounts, Extenders and folding Swing Arms](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/modular-landing-gear.md) with Plates, Skis or Wheels. Wheels roll and steer in Taxi mode; ski-only craft can Taxi on ice. Nearby trapdoor covers open for deployment.
- **Rotate:** [Turntables](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/turntables.md) display builds on floor, wall or ceiling platforms. Redstone controls rotation, direction and Restore; angle and speed sliders set the motion. Adjustable autosnap returns editable blocks when stopped near a quarter turn.
- **Assemble:** build the [Assembly Press, Synthesis Tower and Enclosed Gantry](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/universal-constructors.md) from a Core and Programmable Matter frame. These industrial multiblocks are decorative and do not process recipes yet.
- **Store matter, power and fuel:** a sunlight-powered [Matter Infuser, RF Battery Blocks and Fuel Tanks](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/matter-and-storage.md) support your workshop. Batteries and tanks offer Block/Hexagon shapes and configurable casing; tanks show real fluid levels through their windows and join along their connection axis.
- **Outfit your crew:** [programmable armor](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/programmable-armor.md) includes role designs and complete Pinnacle, Ashiro, Paladyne, Vektor, Seyr and Vandor sets. Matching [Data Cards and Data Chips](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/matter-and-storage.md) are handheld decorative props.
- **Enclose:** [Canopies and adjustable front windows](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/canopies.md) offer sliding/hinged motion, glass or hull materials and redstone control.
- **Move:** [deployable ramps, escalators, stairs, lifts and extending platforms](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/ramp-controller.md).
- **Illuminate:** [programmable lights, light slabs and frames](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/lights.md), with independent on/off artwork and company light designs, plus [thrusters, ion drives, plasma vents, impulse engines and hover effects](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/propulsion.md).
- **Control:** [buttons, switches, levers, sliders, aircraft throttles, screen controls and triggers](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/controls.md), linked by redstone channels carrying levels 0-15.
- **Customise:** edit settings with the [Configurizer](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/CONFIGURIZER.md), copy them with the [Duplifier](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/DUPLIFIER.md), and choose [materials or custom artwork](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/unified-materials.md). Company logos, murals and backgrounds have their own Corp category.

See [what's new in 2.2](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/version-2.2.md)
and the [full changelog](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

Browse the [illustrated block guide](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/gallery/README.md)
and [animated examples](https://github.com/rheide/vandorlabs/blob/master/docs/animated-documentation.md).
Crafting recipes appear in the in-game recipe book. Universal Constructor Cores
and Data Cards/Chips are creative-only. Cockpit consoles and their chair/yoke
peripherals remain work in progress and are temporarily hidden from creative selection.
