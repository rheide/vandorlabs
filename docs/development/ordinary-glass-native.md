# Ordinary programmable glass native boundary

Ordinary programmable glass retains its own settings format, independently of diagonal panel glass. Configured items keep the `ProgrammableGlassSettings` envelope; generic block-entity data is merged before that envelope is applied. Invalid layouts are refused before placement. Picking copies the current settings, while ordinary harvesting yields one unconfigured item. Failed placement rollback retains one inert, recoverable owner.

Player bucket interactions insert and extract contained fluid on either side through the shared contained-fluid interaction handler. Volume checks apply to player insertion; placement captures the original boundary fluid even when that side has no currently visible gap. Ordinary glass deliberately does not implement a vanilla liquid-container interface: dispenser-style insertion remains refused, and flowing water or lava cannot replace its solid glass material.

Default authored frame rails belong to the terrain model. Pane surfaces and selected or bent frames use the block-entity renderer and their separate transparency passes. Both consume the same immutable publication prepared on the owning thread. Geometry, ray tracing and contained-fluid regions remain independent of cosmetic rendering. The canonical registration supplies the completed bake and dedicated menu under the preserved `programmable_glass` block, item, block-entity and menu IDs.

The dedicated editor uses the existing Glass, Front, Interior and Exterior tabs, including frame tiling, reverse option cycling and shared playback controls. Configurizer use and creative sneak-click open the same menu. Every edit rechecks the loaded installed owner, current menu window and held-tool or creative permission; panel facing remains authoritative. Primary edits use a fixed 24-byte transport and frame edits reuse the fixed frame representation.

Inventory selection retains all 304 authored size, shade, tint and frame variants. Only the outer item envelope controls that selection; generic block-entity data and replacement materials do not alter the Source inventory template. Active resource-pack geometry and transforms are retained, full-content atlas UVs are corrected once per static bake, and GUI projection uses the shared slot-padding helper. GUI alpha blending keeps the Source cutoff of 0.1 independently of held-item render types. Copied item meshes share an 8 MiB byte budget; immutable component choices use a bounded weak-identity cache.

Focused canonical acceptance covers Survival placement and harvesting, configured picking, recoverable failed-load owners, old missing-size palettes, dedicated save/restart and the actual dedicated editor. The placed-world client independently disables pane and terrain rendering, checks empty L/T corners and inside rays, and verifies unchanged images after reload. Filled diagonal contacts replay 216 actual Source profiles across every facing, inversion and fill mode. All 304 inventory variants retain visible slot padding before and after reload, including an active resource-pack geometry override. Warmed item selection allocates no heap in the measured 10,000-call batch. These software-rendered checks do not establish hardware shader-pack performance or a full release gate.
