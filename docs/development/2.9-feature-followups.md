# 2.9 feature follow-ups

Forge 1.12.2 remains the working release target. Apply these changes to the experimental native target where the corresponding family is already converted. Deferred native families retain their requirements here. The full release gate is deferred until after user testing; each implemented behavior still needs its relevant focused checks.

Arm-mounted Couplers must preserve articulation rather than make every multi-coupler connection rigid. For couplers arranged along Z, rotation around Z remains possible while the arms telescope as required. Other articulation remains available where the remaining couplers and arm travel allow it. Axial travel is limited by the arms' extension range. Fractional endpoints prevent ordinary block-mode parking; Safe Park must resolve the pose before publishing blocks. Saved ownership, abrupt recovery and ordinary arm endpoint behavior remain acceptance requirements.

Texture additions must retain existing registry/catalog identities where designs are moved or renamed, regenerate the browser and category collages, and verify runtime category consumers and Dynmap mappings. New geometry requires actual rendered and joined-model checks; compilation alone does not establish appearance or performance.

BetterBuildersWands 0.11.1 compares block identity and metadata in its private candidate-selection path. Its public wand/container interfaces do not expose a configured-material predicate. The current Vandor adapter captures settings before use and restores them after raw placement; it cannot remove candidates from the wand's selection or preview. A reliable material-aware implementation therefore needs a selection/preview hook or replacement of that interaction path. This larger compatibility change is reported rather than implemented through temporary world-state or placement-permission tricks.
