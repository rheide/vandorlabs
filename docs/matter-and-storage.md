# Programmable matter and utility storage

Programmable Matter Infusers turn one iron ingot into one Programmable Matter ingot in 200 working ticks. The lens needs an unobstructed view of the sun; night and rain pause processing and fuel consumption. Redstone dust supplies 33 working ticks, and a redstone block supplies 300, matching a vanilla log. A blocked output also pauses fuel use. Insert iron from above, redstone from the sides, and extract the product below with hoppers or item-handler pipes.

Craft the infuser with iron around a glass lens, an Infuser Core in the center, and redstone at the bottom. Infuser Cores are currently available in creative mode only. The former direct crafting recipe for Programmable Matter ingots is removed. Nine ingots craft one Programmable Matter Block; the block can be unpacked into nine ingots.

Battery Blocks hold 100,000 RF and transfer up to 1,000 RF per operation. The green front port accepts energy; the orange rear port supplies energy and pushes it to an adjacent receiver. Other sides are insulated. Right-click for a live charge readout and vertical fullness meter. Rotate placement to choose the input direction.

Hexagonal Fuel Tanks hold 16,000 mB per block and accept registered Forge fluids through their two axial ports. Right-click with a fluid container to fill or empty them. Click the top or bottom of a support for vertical tanks, or a side for horizontal tanks. Connect matching tanks along their axis to share capacity; either end can access the combined contents. Filling starts at the lower end of a vertical stack. Right-click without a fluid container for the combined current/max volume and vertical fullness meter. Different fluids cannot be mixed. Each cell saves its own volume, so splitting a stack preserves the contents of each remaining cell. Mining a tank preserves its contents in the dropped block. Glass windows look into a hollow interior; horizontal hexagonal tanks use broad windows on the four diagonal sides and solid flat top and bottom faces. Stored fluid occupies a three-dimensional volume with a horizontal surface. Horizontal tanks align their hexagonal end faces and ports with the connection axis, like hexagonal thrusters; their cross-section keeps flat sides above and below. The fill height accounts for the changing cross-section so the visible volume matches partial fills. Choose **Shape: Hexagon** or **Shape: Block** in the volume dialog; it applies immediately without changing contents, capacity or connections. Both shapes have a hollow interior, central side windows and a real fluid level. Right-click and the Configurizer open the same dialog, containing the casing picker, shape selector and live contents meter.

The implementations use Forge's built-in `IEnergyStorage` and `IFluidHandler` capabilities, requiring no extra mod. Forge Energy is based on the Redstone Flux API, but machines exposing only the older CoFH interfaces need a compatible bridge. See [Forge's capability documentation](https://docs.minecraftforge.net/en/1.12.x/datastorage/capabilities/).

Seven Data Cards and seven matching Data Chips are available in the creative catalog: unbranded, Pinnacle, Ashiro, Paladyne, Vektor, Seyr and Vandor. They use the supplied perspective item artwork, can be held in hand, and cannot be placed as blocks. Their metadata values are 0 through 6 in that order (`vandorlabs:data_card` and `vandorlabs:data_chip`).
