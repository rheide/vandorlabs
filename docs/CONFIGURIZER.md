# Configurizer

The Configurizer opens a block's existing configuration menu when you
right-click the block while holding the item. It works in survival as well as
creative mode; no sneak key is needed. It opens the settings for programmable
screens and surfaces, portholes, lights, glass, doors, trapdoors, chairs, connected seats,
Landing Gear, and ramp controllers, as well as the redstone-channel menu for switches and propulsion
blocks. Some individual controls, including propulsion Shape, still require
creative mode.

Craft one with a Programmable Matter Ingot (`I`) and one redstone (`R`):

```text
 R
I 
```

The Configurizer edits the clicked block. To copy settings between blocks,
use the [Duplifier](DUPLIFIER.md).

For [programmable armor](programmable-armor.md), hold the Configurizer in the
main hand and armor in the offhand, then shift-right-click into the air to open
the armor picker in survival. Shift-right-clicking a block in this arrangement
copies its displayed face texture to the armor. Armor held directly can also
sample a block face in either game mode without the tool.
