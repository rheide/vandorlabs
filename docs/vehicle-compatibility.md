# Vehicle block compatibility

Vehicle Mode, Turntable Controller and [Copy Controller](copy-controller.md) share one supported-block policy. Each capture validates complete block properties and an exact persistent tile-data round trip; unsupported or lossy data is refused before changing the structure. Vehicle inspection also checks rotation before it allows assembly. An unsupported block leaves the craft and its tile data in the world, and the error identifies its registry name. A mod is never approved wholesale: different blocks in the same mod can have different requirements.

The snapshot retains complete block properties and persistent tile NBT, including inventories, energy, owner information and saved configuration. Foreign machines do not run while carried. Restoring their saved data does not imply that a machine, cable, pipe or external network can function in a vehicle.

Vehicle transfers defer Vandor channel settlement until the complete structure and its control sources have been handed over. This prevents callbacks to removed tiles and temporary signal changes during assembly, parking, or rollback. Another mod's unload hooks, network events, and ordinary neighbor behavior remain that mod's responsibility; passing the tile-free rotation checks is not a sandbox for those effects. For example, an active building material can still react to adjacent fire after placement. Use a dedicated adapter for machines or multiblocks whose behavior depends on world position or neighboring components.

Their private tile types do not receive a blanket adapter. Their ordinary building blocks can pass the tile-free checks above. Entity vehicles supplied by other mods are not blocks and are not captured by this system.

With the checked optional mods installed, Mekanism's replacement for `minecraft:portal` and Malisis Doors' six vanilla fence-gate replacements are refused. These use third-party runtime classes despite their vanilla registry names. Standard Malisis door and trapdoor adapters do not cover fence gates.

The dependency-free checks use the actual registered block and tile classes. In particular, an unknown block's default no-op rotation is insufficient for a directional property. Horizontal facing and axis values must rotate correctly; a complete four-turn cycle must restore the state. Fixed non-cube decorations without an orientation representation are refused. The installed Mekanism energy-cube implementation interprets its side configuration using its facing; Immersive Engineering capacitors store explicit world-side keys. These formats require different adapters.

## Vehicle Controller and pilot seats

Place one Vehicle Controller in the connected craft and at least one complete Pilot Seat. Right-click the controller, use the Configurizer, or sneak-right-click to inspect the craft and assemble it. The captured controller provides parking controls. New assemblies require the controller; older captured craft can still be parked so a controller can be installed.

Pilot Seat dialogs contain independent seat height and automatic Taxi Mode settings and apply changes automatically. Explicit Park/Taxi/Hover channels and redstone trigger settings belong to Vehicle Controller. They do not assemble or park the craft. Multiple Pilot Seats are supported: right-click an empty seat to board it. The first seated pilot controls movement until they dismount, then control passes to the next seated pilot. Each occupant keeps their own seat position and height. Parking transfers seated occupants to their corresponding restored seats.

Autopilot uses a cyan navigation texture, Waypoint an amber route marker, and Vehicle Controller a green chassis symbol.

## Limits and wheels

`vandorlabs-blocks.cfg` has a `vehicles.maxBlocks` setting, default 4096 and range 1â€“32768. It limits new assemblies. Lowering it does not prevent existing vehicles from being restored. The 64-block limit on each axis, 4 MiB snapshot limit and collision-complexity limit still apply.

Small and Medium Wheels occupy one block. Large Wheels reserve a 2Ã—2 vertical footprint. Place a wheel on a vertical side of a slab; the wheel faces outward from that side. Large wheels need free space above and to the right of the placement point when viewed from outside. Wheels count as landing supports, including mixed wheel/landing-gear craft when their actual bottom surfaces have the same height. At least three independent wheel/landing supports or two physical Track blocks are required. Connected tracks count individually. Downward-facing propulsion blocks also terminate discovery and count as supports, so inspection does not follow their exhaust face into the ground. [Vehicle Couplers](couplers.md) can delimit connected vehicle sections. Breaking any large-wheel part removes the whole wheel. In Taxi mode, wheels and landing-gear tyres roll with actual travel, reverse with the craft and swivel with steering. Stopping holds their rotation; Hover centers steering and stops rolling.

Top slabs have an empty lower half. Vehicle discovery does not connect a top slab to a floor beneath that empty space. Slabs that physically touch another block still connect normally.

## Taxi landing height

In Taxi mode, three or more independent downward Extender Arms carrying wheels or tracks can lower the chassis while their feet stay on the ground. Stop and align the craft to the block grid before retracting. Each arm must have one vertical column, share the same height adjustment, and leave a clear, grid-aligned native parking footprint. Connected multi-block track footprints are preserved. Removed arm sections remain in the vehicle snapshot until extending restores them; shortened vehicles can be saved, parked and recaptured. The cockpit Gear control uses the configured redstone channel for both directions.

## Carried paintings

Vanilla paintings supported entirely by a craft are carried through Taxi conversion and restored when parking. Their saved entity data shares the vehicle ownership journal. Geometry preparation uses the original block snapshot, while painting ownership data is validated separately; changing a source tile or painting during queued preparation refuses conversion and retains the native source.

## Carried chest inventories

Right-click a vanilla chest while the craft is in Taxi or Hover mode to open the ordinary chest dialog. Double chests provide the normal 54 slots. Named inventories and vanilla chest locks are preserved. Pickup, insertion and shift-click update the vehicle snapshot, so the contents survive saving and parking. Access follows the actual chest position, and the window closes if the player moves out of reach, the chest is replaced, or an ownership transfer starts.

## Copying and carried ramps

Cockpit reservations save their controller relative to their own position. Structure editors can copy a complete cockpit by relocating the normal tile coordinates without linking the copied controls to the original. Older copies with absolute references resolve only to a nearby cockpit whose authored footprint contains that part; an unrelated distant cockpit cannot own it. Copy the complete footprint, including the seat movement reservation when extended.

Ramp controllers and generated ramp cells save a journal origin. When a clipboard tool changes only the tile's `x`, `y`, `z`, loading translates the private source, controller, source-tile and occupied-cell journals by the same displacement. Internal vehicle translation and rotation update this origin too. This repairs new copies without altering the original ramp. Existing malformed copies made before the journal origin was saved may still require replacing or reconfiguring the copied controller; guessing its original source positions could damage unrelated blocks.

Landing gear performs one deferred footprint check after loading. It recreates missing invisible reserved cells only in air and leaves occupied cells intact. Copy the entire assembly where possible, especially rotated large gear and its covers.

Ramp Controller channels can switch ordinary ramps between their configured endpoints while carried, including capturing an initially retracted platform. The adapter uses the existing platform discovery, material data and tread geometry. It builds a replacement snapshot and checks terrain, occupants, bounds and serialization before applying it. Movement is an immediate endpoint change while carried. If the new geometry is obstructed, the previous snapshot remains intact. Escalator belts remain frozen in vehicle mode.

Survival copying charges one obtainable root item for an IE post or watermill, including its helper cells. An IE Toolbox requires one exact native Toolbox item containing its complete inventory, name and enchantments; its contents are not charged a second time.
