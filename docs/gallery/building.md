# Building blocks, finishes, and glass

For face texture overrides, diagonal portholes, half-height walls and fill
options, see [the 1.1 additions](version-1.1.md).

Programmable Block, Wall, Slab, Stairs, Ramp, and other compatible shapes use a shared finish list. Choose a material with shift-right-click in Creative mode, or use the [Configurizer](../CONFIGURIZER.md) in survival. Some screen blocks use the same list for their wall or side texture while keeping a separate primary screen selection. The [Duplifier](../DUPLIFIER.md) can carry a compatible finish to another shape.

## Choose a form

| Form | Best use |
| --- | --- |
| Programmable Block | Full cube for hulls, floors, and solid trim. |
| Programmable Wall | Thin wall surface with a selectable finish. |
| Programmable Slab | Half-height floor or trim; matching stacked slabs combine into a Programmable Block. |
| Programmable Stairs | Vanilla stair placement and corners, with main and optional per-face textures. |
| Programmable Diagonal Wall | Half/full width, half height, slope direction, and Inside/Outside fill. |
| Programmable Diagonal Porthole | The same proportions with configurable joined openings. |
| Programmable Porthole Wall | Thin wall panel with a glass opening. |
| Programmable Porthole Block | Full-depth porthole for a thick hull. Selected main finish with metal trim around the opening. |

Block, Slab and Stairs have optional per-face texture overrides, disabled by default. Unassigned faces use the main finish.

## Selected close-ups and glass

Portholes offer **Round**, **Hexagon**, **Octagon**, and **Square** openings, glass shade, and **Join**. Joining merges eligible neighboring portholes into a larger window. The wall form is thin; the block form fills the entire block depth.

Programmable Porthole Wall, Programmable Diagonal Porthole and the full-depth Porthole Block have separate **Main**, **Side** and **Glass** texture tabs. Glass offers the same nineteen glass finishes and normal material catalog as Programmable Glass, including trapdoor artwork. **Tint** selects Mild, Normal or Strong glass intensity; for non-glass artwork it selects 35%, 65% or 100% opacity. Main and Side keep their own textures. With **Join** on, **Stretch** fits one image across the joined window opening, while **Tile** repeats artwork per block. Layout controls appear only for non-glass choices.

Hull includes Light Alloy, Dark Gunmetal, and Midnight Satin. Padding includes Seamed, Ribbed, and Stitched. Wall Pipes and Framed Wall Pipes are finishes in the programmable selection rather than separate blocks. Choose them on a full block, slab, or another supported form.

Clear, Pale Cyan, and Smoked Cockpit Glass are separate transparent blocks. Their recipes use eight matching vanilla glass blocks and one Industrial Alloy Ingot to make nine blocks: ordinary glass for Clear, cyan stained glass for Pale Cyan, and gray stained glass for Smoked.

Programmable Glass provides Small or Large detail, nineteen finishes in one **Glass** category, the regular material catalog including trapdoor artwork, a Mild / Normal / Strong tint selector, and Bare / Framed edges. Programmable Diagonal Glass has the same finish, tint and frame choices alongside its slope, size, fill and joining controls.

For example, use Cyan with Mild tint and Bare edges for a clear cockpit canopy, or Dark Grey with Strong tint and Framed edges for a screened observation wall. Tint changes the color overlay opacity while retaining the reflection artwork. Strong uses a 40.5% color overlay for dye colors (Normal 30%, Mild 12%), so the stronger finish remains translucent. Clear has no color overlay, so its three strengths look identical.

For regular materials, Tint controls the artwork opacity instead: **Mild 35%**, **Normal 65%**, **Strong 100%**. Strong makes the panel opaque; texture cutouts remain cutouts. Select any entry in the Glass category to restore the original glass finish. Material selection persists through saves, creative pick-block, configured crafting and Taxi Mode. The Duplifier's **Glass Material / Layout** option copies it independently of shade and tint.

For non-glass choices, **Texture: Tile / Stretch** controls the artwork across joined panels. Tile repeats once per block. Stretch fits one image across a rectangle of matching, coplanar panels. Programmable Glass uses its existing **Join** setting; Diagonal Glass has a material-only **Join** control beside Tile/Stretch, independent of its geometric corner/junction selector. Join Off keeps artwork local to each block. Matching groups use the same material, tint, layout and surface plane; gaps partition a group into complete rectangles. Original glass finishes retain their existing reflection and tint mapping, and never use this layout setting.

Existing regular glass remains Framed with Normal tint; diagonal glass defaults to Framed with Normal tint; saved explicit Bare choices remain Bare. Creative pick-block, configured crafting and the Duplifier preserve both choices. The Duplifier's Frame and Glass Tint options can be disabled independently. These appearance settings do not tick or generate continuing world updates. Diagonal Glass inventory icons always use a visible perimeter frame so clear glass remains recognizable; the placed block still honors Bare / Framed. Joined Bare panels omit their shared internal edge faces to avoid overlapping transparent seams.

Both regular diagonal walls and glass use the same corrected corner geometry.
