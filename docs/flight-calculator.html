Mode readiness

    
What the Vehicle Controller will allow with this build. Every mode also needs a Vehicle Controller, a Pilot Seat and at least three wheel or landing gear supports. Flight needs a Vehicle Status screen to select it.

    

  


  

    
Weight

    
Hover compares lift capacity with the gross weight. Flight uses the effective weight after Antigravity Plates and Repulsor Arrays.

    

    

    

    

  


  

    
Takeoff and climb profile

    
A tick-by-tick run of the Flight controller: Taxi takeoff roll, nose-up at takeoff speed, climb at the chosen pitch, then level at the cruise altitude. Hover over a chart to compare the three together.

Altitude envelope

    
The heaviest effective weight your thrust can hold at each altitude. Where it meets your effective weight is your ceiling. Thrust needed rises by 1 + 24 × (Y − 256) / (100,000 − Y) above Y=256.

Handling

    
Roll, pitch and turn rates scale with the thrust-to-weight response factor, from 1 at the takeoff limit to 4 with four times the thrust needed.
